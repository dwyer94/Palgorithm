Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-07-17

Palgorithm ("the app", "we") is a Palworld breeding-path planner. This policy covers what happens to your data when you use it. Palgorithm is a fan-made tool and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to Pocketpair, Inc. or the Palworld game.

Using the app without an account

By default, Palgorithm requires no sign-in. Your roster, saved plans, teams, and settings are stored only in your browser's local storage, on your own device. We never see this data — it isn't sent anywhere.

What we collect if you create an account

Creating an account is entirely optional and only needed if you want your data to sync across devices. If you do, we store:

  • Your email address, used for sign-in and account-related emails (verification, password reset).
  • The same roster, saved-plan, team, and settings data described above, now synced to our database instead of (or in addition to) your device.
  • If you configure a connection to your own self-hosted live-server proxy, that connection's URL and access token — stored so it can reconnect automatically on your other signed-in devices.

We don't collect payment information (the app is free), analytics, ad-tracking identifiers, or any data beyond what's listed above. We don't sell or share your data with advertisers.

Where your data lives

Account data is stored with Supabase, our database and authentication provider. Each user's rows are scoped so only that user's own signed-in session can read or write them (Postgres row-level security) — we don't manually browse user data, and nobody else's account can see yours.

Account emails (verification, password reset) are sent via Resend, our transactional email provider. Resend only sees the email address and message content needed to deliver that one email — it isn't used for marketing.

Cookies and tracking

We don't use tracking cookies, analytics scripts, or advertising pixels. Supabase's auth library stores your session token in your browser's local storage so you stay signed in between visits — that's the only thing resembling a "cookie" here.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account at any time from the Account tab in the app. This permanently and immediately deletes your account and all associated data (roster, plans, teams, settings) from our database — this cannot be undone. If you only want to stop using the app without an account, you can simply clear your browser's local storage or stop using it; there's nothing else to delete.

Children's privacy

Palgorithm isn't directed at children and we don't knowingly collect data from anyone under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, we'll update the "Last updated" date above.
